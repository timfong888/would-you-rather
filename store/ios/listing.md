## Description (max 4000)
```
One question. Two impossible choices. An instant debate.

Would You Rather turns dinner tables, road trips and boring waiting rooms into real conversation. Pick A or B, see how everyone else voted, then argue about why.

HOW IT WORKS
- Read a dilemma and tap the option you would choose.
- See the vote split from other players.
- Challenge a friend: send a question and see if they pick the same.

NINE CATEGORIES
The High Life, Moral Compass, Midnight Secrets, Social Blunders, Time Traveler, Deep Desires, Career Climber, Tech Dystopia and Wildest Dreams, plus a Kids & Family set. Hundreds of questions, with more added over time.

FREE TO START
Every category has free questions to try. Moral Compass, Social Blunders, Career Climber and Tech Dystopia are free to play, with an optional expansion. Premium categories include a free preview.

UNLOCK WHAT YOU LIKE
- Unlock a single category with a one-time purchase.
- Or unlock every category in one pack, including questions we add later.
- No subscription. Restore Purchases is always available.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account, no sign-up. Your answers are recorded anonymously to show vote totals.

Some categories contain adult themes and are meant for adult and teen players.
```
